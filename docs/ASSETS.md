# Assets and provenance

Everything bundled with Icarus, where it came from, and the terms it is used under.

## Exercise photographs and instructions

- **Source:** Free Exercise DB, https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db
- **Licence:** released into the public domain under the Unlicense (stated in the project's README). The repository's `LICENSE` file was not reachable when the assets were fetched; the README badge and text were used to confirm the dedication. Re-check before relying on it.
- **What is bundled:** 74 photographed movements × 2 frames (start and finish), converted to WebP at a maximum width of 900 px (`src/web/static/media/exercises/`, about 4.3 MB), and their step-by-step instructions (`src/shared/fitness/exercise-instructions.ts`).
- **Changes made:** resized and re-encoded. Some catalog entries use the closest photographed variant; where the equipment differs, the exercise page says so (for example, the goblet squat is photographed with a kettlebell). Two exercises (kettlebell swing, side plank) have no instructions in the source; Icarus wrote its own for those.
- **Reproduce:** `python3 scripts/fetch-exercise-media.py` (needs Pillow and network access to `raw.githubusercontent.com`).

## Gym imagery

Gym cards and galleries use the same Free Exercise DB photographs as **representative images**. Every one is captioned "Representative image from Free Exercise DB (public domain). Not a photo of this gym." A live deployment must replace these with photographs supplied and licensed by each gym.

## People who are not shown

No licensed photographs of children or older adults were available from sources that could be verified, and generated images of people are not used. The audience picker and audience sections are therefore typographic. To add photography:

1. Use images you own or license for commercial use, with model releases. For children, also hold written parental consent for publication.
2. Put them in `src/web/static/media/` (WebP or JPEG; both are served and cached).
3. Record each file here with its source, licence and release.

## Fonts

Cormorant Garamond, Barlow Condensed and Inter, from the `@fontsource` packages, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The licence texts are copied to `dist/web/fonts/LICENSES.txt` at build time.

## Icons and brand mark

Drawn for Icarus in `src/web/ui/icons.ts` and `src/web/ui/brand.ts`.

## Demonstration data

`data/demo/*.json` is generated by `scripts/generate-demo-data.mjs`. Cities and neighbourhoods are real places with approximate centre coordinates. **Every gym, professional, price, offer, policy and qualification is fictional.** Each record has `demo: true`, gyms have no reviews, and every qualification is marked not verified. Names were chosen to be generic; if one matches a real business, that is a coincidence and the name should be changed.
